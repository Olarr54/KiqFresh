Common questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How do I send my shoes to you?

After booking and payment, we email you our postal address and a packaging guide. Postage to us is at your cost — use a padded box and tracked delivery. Once your trainers arrive, we confirm receipt straight away.

Are my shoes insured while they're with you?

Yes. We take full responsibility from the moment they arrive. Our service agreement covers the agreed condition, the service, and the confirmed return — your pair is fully covered.

How long does it take?

Standard turnaround is 5–7 days. Need them faster? Add the express clean (48hr) when booking. We confirm your timeline at booking — no vague estimates.

What types of trainers do you clean?

All of them. Air Force 1s, Jordan 1s, Yeezys, TNs, suede, leather, mesh, canvas. If you're unsure, just message us before booking.
