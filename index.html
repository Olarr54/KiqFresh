FAQ

GOT QUESTIONS?
WE'VE GOT ANSWERS.

How do I send my shoes to you?

After booking, we send you our postal address and a packaging guide. We recommend a padded box and tracked delivery. Once received, we confirm with you immediately.

Are my shoes insured while they're with you?

Yes. We take full responsibility for your shoes from the moment they arrive. Our signed contract outlines the agreed condition, the service, and the confirmed return. So everything is documented and protected.

How long does it take?

Turnaround time depends on the service level and current demand. We give you a confirmed timeline at booking. No vague estimates, just a clear date you can count on.

What types of trainers do you clean?

All of them. Air Force 1s, Jordan 1s, Yeezys, TNs, suede, leather, mesh. We've worked on every silhouette and material. If you're unsure, just message us before booking.
