The 2026 World Cup is here and it's massive. Bigger tournament, more teams, wall-to-wall football for weeks. Which means pub screenings, watch parties, fan zones — and a lot of occasions where you want to look sharp without thinking too hard about it.

Trainers are the obvious choice. Here's what's working right now.

## The classics that always work

**Nike Air Force 1** — Clean, white, versatile. Goes with literally anything. The problem is everyone has them, so the condition of yours matters more. Scuffed Air Forces look worse than scuffed anything else.

**Adidas Samba** — Having a moment that shows no signs of slowing down. The gum sole and green suede tongue give them enough character to stand out, and the low profile keeps them clean-looking even in a busy pub.

**New Balance 550** — The basketball silhouette that became a trainer staple. Wide range of colourways, works equally well dressed up or down, and the leather upper is genuinely easy to keep clean.

**Nike Air Max 90** — A bit of extra height and volume for occasions when you want to make more of a statement. The mesh panels need more care than leather, but the look is worth it.

## Colours to consider for tournament season

If you're going full supporter mode, it depends on who you're backing. For England games, white and navy trainers are the obvious pairing. But honestly, a clean white trainer works as a neutral — it lets the rest of your fit do the talking.

The main thing: whatever colour you wear, wear them clean. Nothing looks worse than a great outfit killed by battered, dirty footwear.

## The practical bit — watch parties are hard on trainers

Pubs and fan zones are rough environments for fresh trainers. Spilled drinks, crowded rooms, outdoor standing areas. Here's how to handle it:

**Before you go**: apply a protective spray. On leather and synthetic uppers this creates a barrier against liquid and light staining. Worth doing the night before, not five minutes before you leave.

**On the night**: if something gets spilled on them, deal with it immediately. A damp cloth or tissue takes most things off before they set. Left overnight, beer and food stains become much harder to shift.

**After**: give them a proper clean before you put them away. Dirty trainers stored in a box hold moisture and get worse, not better.
