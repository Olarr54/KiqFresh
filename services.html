How it works

The postal process.

Simple from start to finish. No fuss.

01
Book online

Choose your service, fill in the booking form, and upload photos of your trainers. We confirm your booking and take payment — then email you our postal address and packaging guide.

02
Post them to us

Pack your trainers following the guide we email you. Postage to us is at your cost — use a sturdy padded box and tracked delivery. We confirm receipt as soon as they arrive.

03
The deep clean

We inspect and treat every pair to the service you chose. Sole scrub, midsole brightening, upper clean, colour restoration. Every millimetre covered.

04
Quality check

Every pair goes through a final inspection. For Elite Restore we send you before/after photos so you can see the transformation before they leave us.

05
Posted back to you

Freshly cleaned, wrapped, and posted back tracked, insured, and confirmed under our service agreement. Spotless at your door.
